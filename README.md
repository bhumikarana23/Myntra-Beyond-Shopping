## 📌 Project Overview

Myntra – Beyond Shopping is a conceptual product extension that explores how Myntra can evolve from a traditional fashion shopping platform into a more comprehensive fashion companion.

The project focuses on enhancing the customer experience beyond product discovery and purchasing by introducing features that support clothing decisions, social shopping, garment care, purchase evaluation and travel packing.

The solution consists of six interconnected features designed around different stages of a customer's fashion journey.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional fashion shopping experiences primarily focus on browsing, purchasing and delivery. However, customers also face challenges beyond the checkout process:

- Understanding fabric composition and clothing suitability.
- Making shopping decisions with friends.
- Maintaining garments and understanding their care requirements.
- Evaluating the long-term value of purchases.
- Planning outfits and packing for different trips.

**Product Opportunity:** Extend the shopping experience into a connected fashion lifestyle ecosystem that supports customers before and after making a purchase.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Myntra – Beyond Shopping introduces six complementary features:

### 1. Fabric – Understand Before You Buy
Provides detailed fabric insights, including material composition, texture, comfort, weather suitability and care instructions, helping customers make informed clothing decisions.

### 2. SquadCart – Shop Together
Introduces a collaborative shopping experience where friends can create groups, shortlist products, share ideas and vote on their preferred choices.

### 3. CareMate – Care Beyond Purchase
Offers garment-specific washing, drying, ironing and maintenance guidance, along with stain-care information and care reminders.

### 4. ValueMeter – Evaluate Your Purchase
Helps customers assess product value through ratings, product attributes, estimated usage and cost-per-wear calculations.

### 5. PackSmart – Smarter Travel Packing
Helps customers prepare for trips through destination-based outfit suggestions, activity preferences and organised packing checklists.

### 6. Home – Your Fashion Dashboard
Brings together personalised recommendations, feature shortcuts and relevant fashion information in one central interface.

## 👤 Target Users

- Online fashion shoppers.
- Customers seeking better product and fabric information.
- Friends who enjoy collaborative shopping.
- Consumers interested in garment maintenance.
- Frequent travellers who need outfit and packing assistance.
